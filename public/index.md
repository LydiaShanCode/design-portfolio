# Lydia Shan

> Product designer in New York City, currently at Adaptive Security. Previously on the payments team at Shopify, designing financial experiences across mobile and desktop, and before that in startups building 0–1 products.

This file is a plain-text map of the site. The pages linked below are the source of truth.

## Selected work

- [Smart Markets](https://lydiashan.com/project/smart-markets): Shopify, 2025. Used AI as a contextual helper to explain why a product is restricted in a market. Support tickets about product restrictions fell by about 18%.
- [Core Workflows](https://lydiashan.com/project/core-workflows): Adaptive Security, 2026. Made campaign setup and testing less nerve-wracking, and shipped two features from brief to launch. In usability testing, 9 of 10 customers said finding Trainings got easier.
- [Level up AICC](https://lydiashan.com/project/level-up-aicc): Adaptive Security, 2026. Gave admins the last word on AI-generated training modules: interactive blocks, the frame editing model, layouts, and narration.
- [Payouts Uplift](https://lydiashan.com/project/payouts-uplift): Shopify, 2025. Made it clearer where a merchant's money comes from and when it arrives. The first milestone, Payout Errors Uplift, reduced payout holds by 43%. Ticket and satisfaction impact are still waiting on launch.
- [Shop Balance](https://lydiashan.com/project/shop-balance): Shopify, 2026. Defined the 0-to-1 North Star for a mobile, buyer-facing balance in the Shop app. Direction-setting work, so there are no launch numbers.
