Shopify Managed Markets · Dec 2025

Why can't I sell this

International expansion is a growth lever. Managed Markets automated the heavy lifting and hid why products were blocked. Merchants felt they had lost control of their inventory. Support filled up with high-priority incidents.

Contextual AI Localization
No, but why is that? Why can't I? Why? What's the reason?

Merchant, concept research

Candles and essential oils were best sellers for one shop, and those items made them unqualified. Another asked if it was volume or dollar amount. The criteria were invisible.

Negotiating the surface

The restriction lived on the most crowded page in Admin

To reopen a restricted product, category metafields have to change. That meant the Product Details Page, owned with other design teams on Publishing and metafields.

The need for visibility

Bring the restriction reason into view so merchants can act.

The concern of clutter

A permanent banner on a page already full of states, including other AI features.

Progressive disclosure

I dropped the loud banner and let a popover do the work

A large alert on the category metafields card would have fought every other state on the page. A popover holds the reason and makes room for Sidekick.

The Guide explains the specific law. The Fixer helps the merchant add the metafields that can clear the restriction.

Why this is restricted

A mid-flight pivot

The publishing flow changed under us. The popover was no longer enough.

Midway through, the Selling Strategies team redesigned the core publishing flow. With a larger footprint I moved the restriction work into a dedicated subpage inside their publishing modal, and kept Sidekick in that new context.

The job stayed the same. The surface did not.

Was it a success

~18%

fewer support tickets on product restrictions after the AI-assisted pattern shipped, as published on my resume.

Fit

The quieter footprint survived other AI states and a publishing redesign. That was the real test.

The case study also names better metafield accuracy from merchants who finally understood the why. I do not have a separate published number for that.

Biggest learning

Never design in a vacuum

Even when you own one piece, you have to know the whole puzzle. Here that meant other AI states and how metafields actually get written. A small footprint meant the work could move when another team rebuilt publishing.
