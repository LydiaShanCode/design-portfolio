From fun money to a real account

Shop Cash felt like a reward you play with. The north star was Shop Balance: a buyer-facing account that works like a debit card, so people can store, spend, and earn inside Shopify. I was the sole designer, with one PM.

0 to 1 Mobile

The old model

Shop Cash was a reward. It was not a place to keep money.

People earned it and spent it on small treats. That was the point, and also the ceiling. The goal of this project was to retire the rewards-only model and give buyers an account they would actually fund.

The product

Store it. Spend it. Load it. Take it out. Report when it breaks.

I designed the end-to-end buyer path in the Shop app: discover and onboarding, pay with Shop Balance, load funds, withdraw, and report an issue. Mobile first. The account had to feel as ordinary as a debit card, not like a coupon drawer.

What I designed

DiscoverOnboarding that explains the account, not the old rewards language.
PurchasePay with Shop Balance at checkout in the Shop app.
LoadAdd funds so the balance is something you keep, not only something you earn.
WithdrawTake money back out. A real account has an exit.
ReportA path to flag an issue when a charge or transfer looks wrong.

Was it a success

This was a north star. I do not have launch numbers to read out.

The case study is the vision and the five flows. The resume names it as the 0 to 1 definition of Shop Balance for storing and spending funds. If I presented this tomorrow I would show the walkthroughs, then say what we would measure: funded accounts, spend share versus Shop Cash, and whether people withdraw.
